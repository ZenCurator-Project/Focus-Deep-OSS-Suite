# 🌿 Focus-Deep OSS Suite

**Reclaim your focus. Minimalist, Open-Source, and Privacy-First.**

Focus-Deep is a suite of productivity tools designed for creators, developers, and deep thinkers who want to eliminate digital distractions and protect their mental space. Our mission is to integrate **Zen philosophy** with modern productivity.

## 🧘‍♂️ Current Module: Zen-Clock
Our flagship minimalist timer. Built on the principle of **"Ichigyo Zanmai" (一行三昧)** — the Zen practice of full immersion in a single act.

### ✨ Key Features
* **Minimalist Dashboard:** A clean interface designed to reduce cognitive load.
* **Privacy-First:** No tracking, no data collection. Your data stays with you.
* **Ethics by Design:** No addictive loops. Just a tool for pure focus.
* **Open Collaboration:** Built for the community, by the community.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Quality
- **Performance:** Lightweight Vanilla JS & CSS3.
- **Security:** 100% client-side execution.
- **Transparency:** Fully open-source under the MIT License.
